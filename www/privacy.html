Privacy Policy — TILT

Effective: 2026-05-02

What we collect

TILT runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect, transmit, or store personal information.

  • No accounts — no name, email, phone, address.
  • No location — no GPS or IP-geolocation.
  • No advertising IDs — no IDFA, GAID, or fingerprinting.
  • No third-party analytics.

What's stored locally

The game uses your browser's localStorage to save your personal best, all-time best, daily streak, mutator collection, and settings (mute on/off). An anonymous session ID is generated each visit for in-browser-only telemetry that never leaves your device.

Clear anytime by clearing site data for this domain.

Cookies

None.

Children / GDPR / KVKK

Suitable for all ages. No personal data collected, so COPPA / GDPR / KVKK consent flows are not applicable. There's nothing to give you, delete, or port.

Changes

If we ever add server-bound telemetry, this policy updates with a clear date and changelog.
